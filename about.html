Who We Are

Nigeria's Emergency Management Agency

NEMA was established by Act 12, as amended by Act 50 of 1999, to manage disasters in Nigeria. It coordinates prevention, preparedness, mitigation and response through a national headquarters and six zonal offices.

Our Mandate

Established to Manage Disasters in Nigeria

The Act vested the authority for managing disasters in NEMA. Under the enabling law the Agency formulates policy on all activities relating to disaster management in Nigeria and coordinates plans and programmes for efficient and effective response at national level.

It also monitors the state of preparedness of all organisations and agencies involved in disaster management, and works through zonal offices, state emergency management agencies, local emergency management committees and partners.

Text adapted from the official About NEMA page on nema.gov.ng.

Mission

To coordinate and facilitate disaster management activities aimed at reducing risks, enhancing preparedness, and ensuring effective response and recovery across Nigeria.

Vision

To build a disaster-resilient Nigeria where communities are prepared, protected, and empowered to withstand and recover from emergencies.

Values

Integrity, Professionalism, Compassion, Collaboration, and Innovation guide every aspect of our disaster management efforts.

Mandate

Established by Act 12 of 1999, NEMA formulates policy on disaster management, coordinates responses, and builds resilience nationwide.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Emergency Management

NEMA's work follows the disaster cycle, from reducing risk before a hazard strikes to supporting recovery afterwards.

Prepare

Public education, simulation exercises and early warning systems that save lives before a hazard strikes.

Mitigate

Risk mapping, GIS analysis and planning that reduce the impact of disasters on communities.

Respond

Search and rescue, relief distribution and coordination of partner agencies during an emergency.

Recover

Post-disaster assessment, rehabilitation support and building back safer communities.

Facilities

How NEMA Coordinates Response

Mission Control Centre

A computer-based satellite facility using the COSPAS-SARSAT system to detect distress alerts from 406 MHz beacons on ships and aircraft, and pass them to rescue coordination centres and military disaster response units.

Geographic Information System Lab

A functional GIS laboratory for early warning and precision response. It collects and analyses spatial data to support disaster risk reduction.

Mobile Clinics and Ambulances

Mobile clinics are stationed in Abuja, Kaduna, Lagos and Port Harcourt, with ambulances in Abuja and all six zonal offices, for deployment during major disasters.

Search and Rescue Helicopter

A search and rescue helicopter is stationed in Abuja and manned by Nigerian Air Force officers, with plans for additional aircraft.

Leadership

Mrs. Zubaida Umar

Director General, NEMA · dg@nema.gov.ng

Under her leadership NEMA has emphasised anticipatory action: turning the Seasonal Climate Prediction into localised risk communication, activating the Fusion and Trigger Room to coordinate action before hazards breach critical thresholds, deepening operational integration with the Nigerian Army, and using simulation exercises to expose and fix response bottlenecks before a real emergency.

Summary based on the NEMA article “How Zubaida Umar is Driving NEMA’s Shift from Reaction to Preparedness”, 24 August 2026.
