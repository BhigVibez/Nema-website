How to Read an Alert

What the Levels Mean

Every alert carries a severity level so you can decide how quickly to act.

● Severe

Act Now

A threat to life or property is imminent or already happening. Evacuate, follow official instructions and call 112.

● Warning

Prepare to Act

Conditions are developing. Secure your home, charge your phone, check on neighbours and monitor updates.

● Information

Stay Informed

Operational updates, relief distribution and guidance. No immediate action is required.
