# Portrait Credits

This folder holds the leadership portraits used on the NEMA standalone site. Files ending in `.svg` are original illustrated placeholders. Files ending in `.jpg` are real photographs published elsewhere; their sources are listed below.

All JPEGs were converted from the downloaded originals by centre-cropping around the face to a square and resizing to 500 × 500 px (JPEG, quality 86, light sharpening). No other retouching was applied.

## Verification

Each JPEG above was checked with the YuNet face detector after cropping: every file contains exactly one detected face, centred in the square crop. No group photograph is used as a final portrait; where a source contained several people, a tight crop around the identified subject was used.

## Rights and reuse

These photographs remain the property of the publishers and agencies named above. They are included here for identification and archival purposes in this prototype. Anyone publishing this site publicly should obtain permission from the rights holder and keep the credit line with the image.

## Still illustrated

Where no reliable, name-verified photograph was found, the original `.svg` placeholder remains. In particular, most current departmental directors and several former directors still use placeholders. Do not replace those files with a search-engine result unless the image can be tied to the named person by its caption, filename or article text.
