NEMA Yola Operations Office Trains NYSC/Emergency Management Vanguards (EMVs), in Yola, Adamawa State

Tuesday, 09 September 2025 1 min read NEMA Press Office
Disaster Risk Reduction operations office

The National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Yola Operations Office, has organized a training session for the NYSC Emergency Management Vanguards (EMVs) serving in Adamawa State, aimed at enhancing their capacity in disaster risk reduction and response.

In his opening remarks, the Head of Operations, Mr. Ladan Ayuba emphasized the vital role of EMVs in disaster management. He urged them to serve as good ambassadors of NEMA in their places of primary assignment and to promote disaster risk reduction across the state.

The training featured two sessions: Introduction to Disaster Management in Nigeria delivered by Mr. Sadiq Nuhu, and Disaster Management Cycle and Roles of EMVs presented by Mr. Pius Ene. The interactive sessions also provided participants the opportunity to engage with facilitators through questions and discussions.

On behalf of the corps members, the EMV president expressed gratitude to NEMA, noting that the training had significantly improved their knowledge and preparedness in disaster management.

9th September, 2025
