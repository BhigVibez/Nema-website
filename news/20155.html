Day 2 Incident Report: 3 Storey Building Collapse at Alagomeji, Yaba, Lagos State

Saturday, 13 September 2025 1 min read NEMA Press Office
Disaster Risk Reduction operations office Search & Rescue

Day 2 Incident Report: 3 Storey Building Collapse at Alagomeji, Yaba, Lagos State

Search and Rescue operation is still ongoing at the building collapsed scene. NEMA, LASEMA, Fire Services, Police, FRSC and NSCDC are on ground.

The number of person rescued remain 4; they are in stable condition. No additional person has been rescued at the time of this report but there is speculation of two more persons under the rubbles.
