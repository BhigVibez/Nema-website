NEMA Kano Operations Office Participates in Multi-Sectoral Emergency Meeting on 2025 Flood Preparedness and Response in Jigawa State

Thursday, 18 September 2025 1 min read NEMA Press Office
Disaster Risk Reduction operations office

The NEMA Kano Operations Office participated in a multi-sectoral emergency meeting on 2025 flood preparedness and response in Jigawa State.

The meeting brought together relevant MDAs and development partners to deliberate on both preparedness and response efforts to the ongoing and anticipated 2025 flood situation. Discussions focused on key needs and proactive measures to strengthen readiness in at-risk communities.

During the session, NEMA Kano Operations Office made a presentation on its preparedness activities so far conducted as part of preparation for the 2025 flood, while some MDAs also presented their respective preparedness and response activities.

– September 16, 2025
