Reception of 145 Nigerian Returnees from Algeria

Tuesday, 23 September 2025 1 min read NEMA Press Office
operations office Search & Rescue

As part of the ongoing Assisted Voluntary Return initiative supported by the International Organization for Migration (IOM) in collaboration with the Federal Government of Nigeria, the Lagos Operations Office of the National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), in conjunction with the National Commission for Refugees, Migrants, and Internally Displaced Persons (NCFRMI), successfully received a total of 145 Nigerian returnees from Algeria on September 22nd, 2025, at approximately 12:15 hours.

The returnees arrived safely at the Cargo Terminal of Murtala Muhammed International Airport, Lagos, aboard an Air Algeria Airlines flight with Registration Number 7T-VKM. The group comprised the following categories:

• 132 Adults
• 9 Children
• 4 Infants

Upon arrival, officials from the Nigerian Immigration Service (NIS) promptly conducted biometric registration and proper documentation for all the returnees to ensure accurate records and smooth reintegration into the country.

In line with the Federal Government’s commitment to the safe, dignified, and humane return of its citizens, the returnees were provided with essential services and immediate humanitarian assistance, including:

• Provision of food and potable water to address immediate nutritional needs
• Medical care and ambulance services to cater for any health-related concerns
• Luggage handling and logistics support to ease their transit process
• General coordination to facilitate a seamless and orderly reception exercise

The collaborative efforts of NEMA, IOM, NCFRMI, and other key stakeholders ensured that the entire reception process was well-organized, efficient, and centered on the dignity and welfare of all returnees.
