DG NEMA Visits Lagos Operations Office During SEMA National Conference

Saturday, 04 October 2025 1 min read NEMA Press Office
Director Generals Office Disaster Risk Reduction operations office

The Director General of the National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Mrs. Zubaida Umar, has paid a working visit to the Agency’s Lagos Operations Office while in Lagos State for the National Conference of State Emergency Management Agencies (SEMAs).

During the visit on 2nd October 2025, the Director General interacted with staff of the office and encouraged them to redouble their efforts in service delivery and commitment to duty. She reminded the officers that their roles are critical in ensuring timely and effective response to disasters in Lagos, stressing that NEMA’s strength lies in the dedication and professionalism of its workforce.

The DG was accompanied by some management staff, including Dr. Onimode Abdullahi Bandele, Director Planning, Research and Forecasting, Dr. Umesi Emenike, Director Human Resources Management,
Alhaji Aminu Boyi Ringim, Director Finance and Accounts, Dr. Ishaya Isah Chonoko, Director Disaster Risk Reduction, Mr. Saheed Akiode, Zonal Director South-West, Hajiya Rakiya Bello Umar Special Assistant to the DG and Mr. Eugene Louis Nyelong, Deputy Director Search and Rescue
They were warmly received by the Head of Operations, Mr. Olatunde Mohammed, alongside the Lagos office staff.

In a brief interactive session, Mrs. Zubaida Umar provided an open platform for staff to freely express their challenges and share suggestions with management. She assured them that their concerns would be given due attention, reiterating that the welfare and professional growth of staff remain a priority of her administration.

The visit formed part of her commitment to ensuring closer engagement with NEMA’s field offices nationwide and to boost morale among personnel delivering services at the frontline of emergency management.

2nd October 2025
