DG NEMA Attends Aatf Meeting as Vice President Directs Activation of Early Action Platform for Flood Response

Friday, 26 June 2026 1 min read NEMA Press Office
Director Generals Office

The Director General of the National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Mrs. Zubaida Umar, on Thursday attended the meeting of the Anticipatory Action Task Force (AATF) presided over by the Vice President, Senator Kashim Shettima, at the Presidential Villa, Abuja.

The high-level meeting focused on strengthening Nigeria’s preparedness for the ongoing flood season through enhanced coordination, early warning, and anticipatory action.

During the meeting, Vice President Shettima directed the immediate activation of the Fusion and Trigger Room concept within NEMA as a national platform for coordinating early action against flooding and other climate-related disasters.

Emphasising the need to move beyond reactive disaster response, Senator Shettima stated that the administration of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu remains committed to proactive preparedness, stressing that government must act before disasters occur in order to reduce losses and protect vulnerable communities.
He said Nigeria requires a robust institutional mechanism capable of anticipating hazards, consolidating data, coordinating decisions, and triggering timely actions before emergencies escalate.

Earlier, the Minister of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction, Dr. Bernard Doro, described the Federal Government’s approval of funds for anticipatory flood action as a bold and timely intervention.
Also speaking, the United Nations Resident and Humanitarian Coordinator in Nigeria, Mr. Mohamed Fall, commended President Tinubu and the Federal Government for approving the intervention fund, describing the decision as a demonstration of political commitment, national ownership, and practical leadership in disaster risk management.

On her part, the Director General of NEMA, Mrs. Zubaida Umar, noted that the action taken by the Federal Government would significantly contribute to preventing loss of lives and protecting livelihoods across vulnerable communities.

She informed the meeting that NEMA had already prioritised high-risk areas for intervention and had earlier issued flood early warning advisories to support preparedness and risk reduction efforts across the country.

It will be recalled that the National Economic Council (NEC) recently approved an intervention fund to support anticipatory actions aimed at mitigating the impact of flooding across Nigeria.
